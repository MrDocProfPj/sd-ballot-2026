Before you go

  • BringPhoto ID: SD license or ID, passport, military or tribal ID, or current SD school photo ID. No ID? Sign an affidavit and vote normally.
  • WhereLook up your polling place →
  • Vote earlyClosed Monday at 5 p.m. Vote at your polling place Tuesday.
  • Mail ballotHand it to the County Auditor. It must reach your precinct by 7 p.m. Tuesday; postmarks don't count.
  • Good to knowNo write-ins. No straight-ticket option. Skipping a race is fine. Marked the wrong oval? Ask for a new ballot.
  • QuestionsMinnehaha County Auditor 605-367-4220
  • VermillionAll city precincts vote at the National Guard Armory, 603 Princeton St. Don't leave a mail ballot in the courthouse drop box; the county says those won't count.
  • QuestionsClay County Auditor 605-677-7120 Courthouse, 211 W Main St
