# Vermillion and Clay County ballot research

Research cutoff: November 2, 2026. Scope: a voter at 414 E Clark St in Vermillion, which the requester independently confirmed is in Legislative District 17.

## What is different on this ballot

- **State Senate, District 17:** one certified candidate, incumbent Republican Sydney Davis. She still appears on the general-election ballot. The Secretary of State's omission rule applies specifically to **unopposed county candidates**, not legislative candidates, and the certified list gives Davis ballot order 1 in District 17. ([SD Secretary of State certified candidate list](https://vip.sdsos.gov/candidatelist.aspx?eid=774))
- **State House, District 17:** vote for up to two among Chris Kassin (R), Troy Redler (R), Austin Brunick (D), and Leslie Gerrish (D). ([Clay County election guide](https://theheartlandcommunityledger.com/clay-county-voting-information/#district-17-candidates), [certified field and primary results](https://ballotpedia.org/Leslie_Gerrish))
- **County:** the only county contest with more than one filed candidate is County Commissioner At Large: Geoffrey Gray-Lobe (D) and David Thiesse (R). Both are incumbents elected together in 2022 to four-year, at-large seats; the 2022 official ballot said “vote for 2.” This means the 2026 line is best understood as **vote for up to two for two seats**, with two candidates, so it is printed as a contest but offers no head-to-head choice. ([2026 candidate field](https://spectrumvote.com/sd/clay-county/clay-county-commissioner-at-large), [2022 official Clay County ballot/results](https://www.claycountysd.org/userfiles/files/Auditor%20and%20Elections/Carri_Crum_001.pdf), [2022 filing notice: two seats, four-year terms](https://claycountysd.org/userfiles/files/Auditor%20and%20Elections/Website%20Primary%20Candidate%20List.pdf))
- **No Nov. 3 Clay County or Vermillion local measure was found.** Clay County's 0.5% sales/use-tax referendum is a separate election on **Nov. 17**, not part of the Nov. 3 general ballot. ([Clay County November election notice](https://theheartlandcommunityledger.com/clay-county-voting-information/), [county meeting record confirming the separate referendum](https://www.noticeregistry.com/south-dakota/notice/public-hearing-vermillion-248857))

## District 17 political context

- District 17 has genuine Democratic history compared with most of South Dakota. Democrat Tom Jones won its Senate seat by about five points in 2012, and Democrat Ray Ring held one House seat from 2013 through 2020. Republicans have held the Senate seat since Arthur Rusch defeated Michelle Maloney in 2014, and they have held both House seats since the 2020 election. ([2012 Senate result](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Dakota_State_Senate_elections%2C_2012), [2014 Senate result](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Dakota_State_Senate_elections%2C_2014), [Ray Ring's legislative service](https://www.ktiv.com/2024/10/08/decision-2024-election-profile-south-dakota-house-district-17-candidate-ray-ring/), [2020 House result](https://ballotpedia.org/Sydney_Davis))
- The USD/Vermillion part of the district remains noticeably more Democratic than Union County. In the 2024 House race, Clay County gave Democrat Ray Ring 2,716 votes, more than Republican William Shorma's 1,837 and close to Kassin's 2,784; districtwide, however, Kassin won 40.7%, Shorma 33.4%, and Ring 25.8%. ([official Clay County 2024 worksheet](https://www.claycountysd.org/userfiles/files/Auditor%20and%20Elections/General%202024%20ELECTION%20NIGHT%20WORKSHEET.pdf), [certified districtwide 2024 result](https://ballotpedia.org/South_Dakota_House_of_Representatives_District_17))
- The post-2021 district combines Clay County with Republican-leaning southern Union County communities including Dakota Dunes, North Sioux City, Elk Point, and Jefferson. A model based on 2024 presidential results rates the current lines about **R+19** on the two-party vote, so 2026 is more competitive in candidate choice than 2022 or 2024, when only one Democrat ran for two House seats, but Republicans still begin with a districtwide advantage. ([district communities](https://lily-bellflower-w8zd.squarespace.com/), [2022 House result](https://www.multistate.us/elections/results/south-dakota-2022), [2024 House result and partisan lean](https://99chambers.app/sd/house/17))

## Local, school, judicial, and special-district questions

- **No Nov. 3 city or school race:** Vermillion held its municipal election on June 2, including mayor, Southeast Ward council, and a Home Rule Charter amendment. The city's official results show the amendment passed 1,288 to 381. Clay County's June 2 worksheet also includes the Vermillion School Board race, confirming those elections were consolidated with the primary rather than the Nov. 3 general election. ([official city election page](https://www.vermillion.us/239/Election-Information), [official city canvass](https://www.vermillion.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_06152026-1236), [official county June 2 worksheet](https://www.claycountysd.org/userfiles/files/Auditor%20and%20Elections/Primary%202026%20ELECTION%20NIGHT%20WORKSHEET.pdf))
- **No Nov. 3 local measure found:** the county's only November local question is Ordinance 2026-05 at the separate Nov. 17 special referendum. A “yes” approves a 0.5% county gross-receipts and use tax effective July 1, 2027; a “no” blocks it. ([Clay County November election guide](https://theheartlandcommunityledger.com/clay-county-voting-information/))
- **No judicial-retention question found:** the Secretary of State lists the five Supreme Court terms as expiring in 2028, 2030, 2032, 2030, and 2030, so none is scheduled for a 2026 retention vote. ## Locally notable issues

- **Data centers and local control:** Vermillion and Clay County spent late summer considering data-center zoning after talks about a possible project. Residents raised water, power, noise, light, land, utility-rate, housing, and tax-incentive concerns; the county sent a zoning rewrite back to Planning and Zoning after public opposition. Brunick and Gerrish favor a moratorium and signed D-CAP; Thiesse is the commission representative on the relevant planning bodies. ([USD Volante coverage](https://volanteonline.com/2026/09/clay-county-discusses-potential-data-center/), [county agenda reporting](https://citizenportal.ai/feed/55676), [D-CAP commitments](https://drgnews.com/2026/08/28/some-south-dakota-legislative-candidates-sign-data-center-accountability-pledge-urge-other-candidates-to-do-so-as-well/), [planning roster](https://www.claycountysd.org/zoningcounty.cfm))
- **County budget and the Nov. 17 tax vote:** the commission approved a reduced 2027 budget after debate over non-mandated community services, reserves, bridges, flood exposure, employee pay, and whether a new county sales tax should fund property-tax relief. Gray-Lobe and Thiesse took visibly different tones in that debate, summarized in their entries above. ([county meeting record](https://www.noticeregistry.com/south-dakota/notice/public-hearing-vermillion-248857), [county-agenda digest](https://citizenportal.ai/feed/55676))
- **USD connection:** Kassin works for the USD Foundation, and the data-center debate has included concerns about student rents and utilities. The Board of Regents itself is not an elected office on this ballot.
